GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A few quick details about the CLOUDY learning space.

What is CLOUDY? ＋
CLOUDY is a study website concept that brings learning tools such as an AI tutor area, notes, slide creation, quizzes and focus sessions into one place.
Can I use it on a phone? ＋
Yes. This design is responsive, so the layout adjusts for phones, tablets and computers.
Does the AI Tutor work with a real AI right now? ＋
Not yet. This is the visual website design preview. A real AI service needs a secure backend connection before it can answer live questions.
Will my notes sync across devices? ＋
Online accounts and cloud syncing are future features. They need a database and sign-in system to work safely.
